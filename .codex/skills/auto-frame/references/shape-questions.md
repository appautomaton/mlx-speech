# Shape Questions

The canonical diagnostic questions per work shape, stated once for both mode diagnostics. When the shape is not feature, these take priority and mode questions fill the remaining gaps. For mixed work, ask the highest-priority question from each constituent shape.

- **Parity:** What is the reference system or benchmark? Which gaps matter most: paying customers, compliance, or a performance bar? What does "closed" mean: matching output, passing tests, or meeting the bar? Which gaps are safe to close now versus blocked?
- **Audit:** What questions must the audit answer? What decision depends on the findings? What evidence would change the next move?
- **Refactor:** What structural problem are you solving? What behavior must remain invariant? What is the blast radius, and how do you verify nothing broke?
- **Migration:** What are the source and target states? What compatibility constraints exist? What is the rollback plan?
- **Coverage:** What risk areas are undertested? Where have bugs escaped? What is the target improvement, and which test types close it?
